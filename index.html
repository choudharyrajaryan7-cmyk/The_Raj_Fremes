About Me

I don't just edit —
I craft emotions

Hi, I'm Raj — a video editor, photo retoucher, and motion graphics artist who believes every frame should earn its place. I work with YouTube creators, growing brands, and couples who want their wedding day to feel alive every time they replay it.

My editing style leans cinematic but stays human. Whether it's a 60-second reel that needs to stop the scroll, a brand video that builds trust, or a wedding film that makes your mother cry — I bring the same obsessive attention to pacing, color, and sound.

Based in India. Available worldwide. Fast turnarounds. No fluff.
